How does it work?

  1. Places are dots and roads are lines with a length: a graph stored as an adjacency list.
  2. Dijkstra's algorithm with a min-heap (priority queue) finds the shortest path, always picking the closest unvisited place next.
  3. The van goes to the nearest remaining stop each time; the result is compared with a simple A, B, C order.
